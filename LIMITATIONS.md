## What You Need to Do

### Manual Steps Required:

1. **Download MASM32**
   - Visit: http://www.masm32.com/download.htm
   - Download: masm32v11r.zip
   - Extract to your system

2. **Install MASM32**
   - Run: install.bat (from extracted folder)
   - Install location: C:\masm32\

3. **Add to PATH**
   - Add: C:\masm32\bin to your system PATH
   - Restart terminal

4. **Build & Run**
   - `cd d:\COAL`
   - `build.bat`
   - `bin\interpreter.exe`
